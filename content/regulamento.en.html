Introduction

The main aim of the league is for all the drivers taking part to get together and have a good time, and what matters most is respect on and off the track.

The general idea of the league is to have fun doing a sport we enjoy, at affordable prices, with healthy competition and a sense of camaraderie.

The league is run according to these rules, which every participant agrees to respect simply by registering.

Registration

Registering for the league

To register for the league, a driver must provide:

  • Name
  • Email
  • Phone number
  • (Optional) Photo
  • (Optional) A description for the drivers page

Registering for the league costs €3, which goes towards the prizes.

Registering for a race

To enter a race, we ask drivers to confirm they are coming at least two weeks before the event. This gives us an idea of the number of drivers, so we can book the race.

Entering a race has no extra cost, only the kart and track hire.

Calendar

The league is made up of races. Each season's dates and tracks are on the races page.

Drivers choose the date of each race by voting in the WhatsApp group.

The track is drawn by an automatic system that awards points to a list of candidate tracks over the course of a day. The track with the most points at the end of the draw is chosen. If that track is not available on the chosen date, the track with the second most points is used.

After the league ends, there will be a get-together lunch, which includes the prize-giving ceremony.

Points

Points per race

Points for each race are awarded as follows:

  • One extra point goes to the driver with the fastest lap.

DNS, DNF and DSQ

Drivers who do not take part in a race are scored a DNS (Did Not Start).

Drivers who take part in a race but do not finish it are scored a DNF (Did Not Finish).

Drivers who are disqualified from a race are scored a DSQ (Disqualified).

Disqualifications are only given in extreme cases: the reasons for disqualification are listed in the conduct section.

Overall standings

From the third race of the championship onwards, each driver's worst score is dropped from the overall standings. A disqualification (DSQ) cannot be dropped.

This rule is meant to reduce the impact of a driver missing a race (DNS) or having bad luck such as a mechanical problem (DNF).

If the driver set the fastest lap in the dropped race, they still keep the fastest-lap point.

Tie-break

If drivers are tied in the final league standings, the tie is broken by the number of first places each of them has. If they are still tied, the number of second places, then third places, and so on, is used.

Teams

Drivers can form teams of two. Teams must be formed before the start of the third race of the league. To do so, the team members must provide:

  • Team name
  • Names of the two drivers in the team

To keep teams balanced, each team has a budget of 5 tostões. Drivers who finished last season in the TOP 5 cost 3 tostões. All other drivers cost 2 tostões. Drivers who did not take part last season also cost 2 tostões.

(The idea is to stop the two fastest drivers teaming up and sweeping the lot.)

Team standings

A team's score is the sum of its drivers' scores in the overall standings.

Race format

Karts

Dry races are run in 390cc karts, whenever they are available. Wet races are run in 270cc karts.

Race format

  • 5 minutes of free practice (or more, if everyone agrees).
  • A 15-minute race.

Starting grid

The starting grid is set in reverse order of the overall standings. If two or more drivers have the same points, the following criteria are used:

  1. Number of races attended: the driver with more races starts ahead.
  2. Number of 1st places, 2nd places, 3rd places, …: the driver with the weaker record starts ahead.
  3. If they are still tied after the criteria above, the order is decided by drawing lots.

Examples:

  • Driver A with 10 points from 2 races, Driver B with 10 points from 1 race: Driver A starts ahead of Driver B (criterion 1 applies).
  • Driver C with 3 points from 1 race (15th place), Driver D with 3 points from 1 race (16th place): Driver D starts ahead (tied on criterion 1, criterion 2 applies).
  • Driver E with 7 points from 2 races (14th and 15th), Driver F with 7 points from 2 races (15th and 14th): drawing lots (tied on criteria 1 and 2, criterion 3 applies).

In other words, the starting grid is not set by practice times. The exception is the first race, where the grid is set by practice times.

Ballast

By default, ballast is used whenever the track offers it. If the price is unreasonable, the group votes in WhatsApp for or against using it.

Prizes

Overall standings

Trophies go to the top 3 drivers in the league.

Team standings

A trophy goes to the team that finishes first.

Lucky raffle

Every race a driver takes part in earns one raffle ticket. At the end of the championship, the winning ticket is drawn. The winner gets two bottles of Quinta de Cottas™ red wine.

Conduct on and off the track

The general idea of the league is to have fun doing a sport we enjoy, at affordable prices, fighting for the best possible position, but ALWAYS RESPECTING our rivals, whatever their position on track or their experience. So we ask all drivers for COMMON SENSE and FAIR PLAY.

Conduct on track

We want to call attention to certain behaviour:

  1. Do not tap the back of the driver in front of you, especially on the way into corners.
  2. Do not overtake with the “shoehorn” move, that is, passing a driver on the inside of a corner while leaning on the driver on the outside.
  3. Do not zig-zag on the straights to stop someone overtaking.
  4. Respect the start signal.
  5. Respect the general rules of the track and of karting.

Protests

If a driver wants to protest someone's behaviour on track, they must send an email to formula88.stewards@outlook.com with the subject “PROTESTO - [Track]” (the subject really must say “PROTESTO”).

The email must also include video clips that clearly show the incident, and name the drivers involved. Protests can be made within 1 week of the race date.

The stewards will not spend time looking for the right onboard video or the right lap of the incident. It is the protesting driver's responsibility to provide the relevant videos, cut down to just the relevant moments.

The stewards will review the protest and make one of the following decisions:

  • No penalty
  • Warning
  • 3-second penalty
  • 5-second penalty
  • 10-second penalty
  • 15-second penalty
  • Disqualification from the race

If a penalty is given, a member of the organisers will announce it in the WhatsApp group.

Disqualifications

If a driver commits a serious and clearly retaliatory act, whether on or off the track, it may lead to the driver being disqualified from the race and/or the league.

If a driver confirms they are coming to a race and does not show up, without any warning, they are considered disqualified from that race, and cannot drop it from the final standings.
